- **Business-rules audit (2026-08-12, user-requested, no new Banani screen)** — full read-through of every average/coefficient/evaluation calculation across `grades.ts` + the 3 endpoints that compute an average (`students/[id]/results`, `class-subjects/[id]/notebook`, `classes/[id]/notebook`). Two real, high-severity bugs found and fixed:
  1. **`Evaluation.maxScore` was never normalized before weighting.** All three endpoints fed `g.score` (the raw stored value) straight into `weightedAverage`, so an evaluation graded out of 10 and one graded out of 20 got averaged together with no rescaling — mathematically meaningless (a "16/20" and an "8/10" are both 80%, but averaging the raw numbers 16 and 8 produces neither). `maxScore` is a free per-evaluation field (`EvaluationConfigForm`, no restriction to 20), so this was reachable in normal use, not theoretical. Fixed by extracting the three duplicated local functions (`scoreOf`/`computeAverage`/`subjectAverage` — itself a latent drift risk, one place to update, three actually updated historically) into a single `subjectAverageFor()` in `grades.ts` that rescales every score to `(score / maxScore) * 20` before weighting by the evaluation's own coefficient. Backward-compatible: when `maxScore` is 20 (the default, and what all seed data uses) the formula is a no-op, identical output to before.
